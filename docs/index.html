About CLIP TRIBUNE

A Windows desktop video editor for short clips, split-screen compositions and user-provided overlays. Optional integrations upload selected videos to YouTube and TikTok, subject to platform permissions. An opt-in live-stream series can also send generated clips to either or both platforms without per-clip confirmation. The TikTok mode is experimental and is not represented as App Review compliant. The app is maintained by matthew-mbn and is independent of those platforms.
